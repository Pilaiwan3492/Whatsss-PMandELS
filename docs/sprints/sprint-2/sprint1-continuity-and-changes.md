## 4. Approved Baseline Revisions & Rationale Summary

1. **Preservation of Original Sprint 1 Scope (`F-01`..`F-09`) and Sprint 2/3 Allocation (`F-10`..`F-19`):**
   - No Sprint 2 feature (`F-10`..`F-16`, `F-19`) has been deferred or dropped. All 8 planned Sprint 2 features and both planned Sprint 3 features (`F-17`, `F-18`) retain their exact feature IDs and requirement mappings from the Deliverable 1 baseline (`docs/features.md`, `docs/sprints/plan.md`, `docs/traceability.md`).
2. **Lab 6 Security Hardening (`DEF-OUT-01`):**
   - Hardened `roleGuard.ts` to perform case-insensitive route boundary checks, strengthening `BR-10` and `AC-02-2` without altering any functional requirement.
3. **Lab 7 Business Rule Evolution (`BR-07` $\rightarrow$ `BR-07 v1.1`):**
   - Evolved `F-07` (`FR-09`) with `AC-07-7`..`AC-07-10` to prevent micro-repayments (`< 500.00 THB` or `< 10%`), protect a `100.00 THB` partial-repayment cash buffer, allow full settlement of small debts, and reject zero-debt repayments (`422 ZERO_OUTSTANDING_DEBT`), while preserving the original Golden Journey (`3,000.00 THB` full repayment from `30,000.00 THB` cash) and `BR-08` expense exclusion.
